Core Values

The Eden House Public School is commited to

Climate of love and care

We enhance our learning environments and recognize the strengths and needs of all students through an ethos of caring for oneself, others, and the community.

Honouring the Whole Child

We focus on all aspects of a child's education, including academic growth, extracurricular pursuits, emotional intelligence and resilience, and physical well-being

Innovative instruction

We offer academic experiences that inspire all students to build agency and fully engage in learning

Equity and social justice

We are commited to access, equity, diversity, anti-racism, human rights and participation in our community.
